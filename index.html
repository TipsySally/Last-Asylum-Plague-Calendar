Survival Battle

Runs every day, 6 sub-objectives on a 4-hour rotation, cycling across a 7-day pattern. The live day is calculated automatically — no saving needed. Click any day below to preview it; click "Today" to jump back.

New to this event? Start here
What it is

A personal scoring event that runs continuously in 4-hour blocks, each with its own objective. You are ranked against other players in your own level bracket, not the whole kingdom — the panel shows your current range (e.g. Lv.23–24).

How to score

Only actions matching the current block's objective score. During a Technology Research block, for example, the in-game panel lists: use 1 min of research speedup +10, raise Tech Might by 1 +1, buy a pack containing diamonds +30.

The one habit that matters
  • Bank, then dump. Hoard speedups, resources and upgrade materials, then spend the lot inside the matching 4-hour window. Spending the same items outside the window scores nothing.
  • Check the rotation above so you know which block is next — it repeats on a 7-day cycle, and the objective changes every 4 hours.
  • Reward stages are claimed separately from your rank rewards. Claim them as you pass each threshold.

Alliance Duel

Runs Monday–Saturday against a matched rival alliance. One themed phase per day; each day's result settles at server midnight (00:00 GMT−2), and whoever wins more of the 6 phases wins the week. No Alliance Duel activity on Sunday.

New to this event? Start here
What it is

Your alliance versus a matched rival alliance, Monday to Saturday, one theme per day. Nothing runs on Sunday. Each day settles at 00:00 server time.

How to score

Do the tasks listed for that day. The exact tasks and their point values are in the day-by-day list above — open the day you are on.

Where the points actually are

The values are wildly uneven, so ordering matters more than effort:

  • Send a UR Caravan — 220,000. The single biggest line in the week.
  • Execute a UR Covert Operation — 168,750.
  • Complete a Falcon Quest — 25,500.
  • Consume a UR Hero Shard — 20,500, Raven Essence 5,125, Study Scroll 615.
  • By contrast, gathering 100 grain is 10.2 and using a minute of speedup is 122.5. Do the big ones first; the small ones are filler.
Common mistakes
  • Spending speedups on the wrong day. Construction speedups score on building days, research on Wednesday, training on Friday — check the theme before you burn anything.
  • Forgetting that switching alliance mid-duel forfeits your alliance points for that day.

Alliance League

A seasonal ladder layered on top of the Alliance Duel, with promotion and relegation between groups.

New to this event? Start here
The 30-second version

A season-long ladder sitting on top of the Alliance Duel. The top 16 alliances in a kingdom are grouped, then promoted or relegated on victory count.

If you do nothing else
  • You must personally earn a minimum point total during the season or you claim nothing, however well the alliance places.
  • Only kingdoms open more than 7 weeks take part, so newer servers may have no league at all.

How the League works

Source
Source: community “Alliance League” rules infographic, 2026. Not verified in-game. English-only for now.
  • Only kingdoms that have been open for more than 7 weeks take part. If too few kingdoms qualify, some may have no league that round at all.
  • Within a kingdom, only the top 16 alliances can join. Where there are not enough alliances of the same rank to form a group, the remainder are matched across ranks.
  • Ranking is by number of victories. Ties are broken by who won earlier; if both victories and timing tie, the order is randomised.
  • At the end of each league period the top 16 are ranked by victory count and win order, then promoted or relegated on in-group standing. Rank changes take effect the following period.
  • Groups observed: Elite, Epic and Mythic. Rewards are banded for No.1, No.2–3 and No.4–16, with separate personal bands at 1–10, 11–30 and 31–100.
  • Rewards are not automatic. A player must personally earn a minimum point total during the season to claim anything; the observed panel showed a threshold in the low millions of points. League rewards pay out when each event period ends.

The 30-second version

Two hero ladders, neither of which feeds KvK. Arena of Fate takes the top 1,200 by hero might; Triangular Arena the top 200. Battles consume no troops.

If you do nothing else
  • Check Arena of Fate first. Its Intermediate bracket reaches down to might rank 1,200, so most active players qualify for something.
  • Like the top 3 three times a day for 30 diamonds each. It is free and takes seconds.
  • Losing costs nothing but the challenge, so there is no reason to leave daily attempts unspent.

  • Attack and defence lineups are saved separately, so configure both rather than reusing one.

  • Rewards come as ranking, daily and alliance rewards; alliances with members in the top 10 receive extra.

One weekly cycle runs Sunday through Saturday. Times below convert automatically to your selected timezone.

What it is

Kingdom versus kingdom, run weekly. Your kingdom accumulates Kingdom Points through the week, the two sides are ranked, and then one attacks the other.

What earns Kingdom Points

Ordinary events, played normally: Alliance Duel, Demon King, Elixir Scramble and Survival Battle rankings all feed the kingdom total. You do not need to do anything special — playing the week's events is the contribution.

The part to actually diarise
  • Friday 12:00 server time. Royal City and Turret protection drops and the Royal City Scramble begins. This is where the week is decided — see the timeline above converted to your local time.
  • Higher score becomes the attacker, lower becomes the defender. A tie goes to the earlier kingdom in server order.
  • The attacker can relocate into the defender's kingdom once roles lock.
  • At the end, everyone is relocated home, rankings lock and rewards arrive by mail.

Alliance rules — Saturday

Two standing instructions for members on Saturday, when enemy kills score and your territory is a target all day.

  1. Keep your shield up all day Saturday, until server reset. Drop it only for the moments you are personally attacking, and put it straight back. An unshielded city on kill-event day is free points for the other side.
  2. Do not gather from resource tiles at all on Saturday. Gathering troops sit outside your city, and a shield does not protect anything outside the walls — a march on a farm is an undefended target that also tells an attacker exactly where your army is. Whatever you would have gathered is worth less than the troops.

Elixir Scramble

Runs every Friday, server time, with two scheduled slots.

New to this event? Start here
The 30-second version

Two alliances fight for buildings for 30 minutes. Buildings generate points every second while you hold them. Most points at the end wins.

If you do nothing else
  • Follow the commander. Roaming alone gets you wiped and costs the alliance more than you gain.
  • Garrison what you take. An empty building is retaken in minutes.
  • Low might is fine. Defending, taking camps and collecting Medkits all score. The event provides its own troops — no preparation needed.

Phase 1 — registration & matching

  • R5/R4 pick the roster during registration: up to 20 starting members and 10 reserves. Starting members get entry priority.
  • Once registration closes the participant list is locked — leaving the alliance afterwards does not remove you.
  • Opponents are matched on the combat ability of the assigned members, using the highest historical Might of all participants.

Phase 2 — pre-battle preparation (5 min)

  • Starting members enter first, into the left and right entry zones, where they cannot be attacked. Your side is blue, the enemy red.
  • Organise troop formations in advance, stay inside the entry zone, and wait for the commander before moving.
  • Do not relocate your sanctuary outside an entry zone — anything outside can be attacked.

Phase 3 — the battle

Early (0–10 min) — open: Alchemy Workshop, Observation Platform, Migration Portal, Healing Tent. Send the main troop straight to the Alchemy Workshop; auxiliaries take the other three. Station troops at anything captured to hold it.

★ Mid showdown (10–13 min) — open: Elixir Castle, War Relics, Cursed Altar. Everything rallies onto the Elixir Castle; losing it is a strategic disadvantage. Assign separate squads to War Relics and Cursed Altar. Scattered movement is forbidden in this phase.

Late (13–30 min) — open: Camps, Medkits. Garrison and hold; do not leave a post without the commander. Camps are for lower-might members using auxiliary troops, keeping main forces on the key buildings. When Medkits appear, send a small auxiliary unit for them — a large number drop around any building that changes hands.

First-timer pitfalls

  • Don't move randomly. Acting alone gets your troops wiped and disrupts alliance strategy.
  • Always garrison after capturing. An undefended point can be retaken within minutes, costing a lot of Medkit points.
  • Don't abandon buildings late. Chasing camps while neglecting defence is the classic first-timer score drop.
  • You don't need to fight head-on. Defending points and capturing camps earns substantial alliance points.

FAQ

  • My might is low — can I still take part? Yes. Defending, capturing camps and collecting Medkits all score. Active mid- and low-might members often out-contribute high-might members who don't show up.
  • Do I need to train soldiers first? No. The event provides its own troops; tier is set by the highest soldier level unlocked in your Training Grounds. Injured troops heal by occupying Medical Tents and are claimed from the green house icon, bottom-left of the event UI.
  • Must I win every fight to capture a building? No. Small early buildings can be taken when enemy presence is low; for major buildings such as the Castle, move with the main force.
  • What rewards are there? Both alliance and personal. Winning grants extra alliance rewards; everyone gets participation rewards, scaled by buildings captured and camps occupied.

Event guide — how the valley works

Source
Source: two community “Crystal Cluster Valley Event Guide” infographics, which agree with each other throughout. The 30-minute window and the 20-minute entry cut-off match what this page already had. Not verified in-game. English-only for now.
  • You are given troops on entry. On entering you receive troops equal to your Barracks capacity, all at your highest unlocked tier — so your own army is irrelevant and low-might players are not disadvantaged on numbers.

How the rotation works

  • Terms last five minutes.
  • “Time in office” on each panel counts down to the handover, not up from the appointment.
  • Each title has an Auto Appointment List capped at 50. Press Apply to join the queue; it then cycles without further input.
  • A long remaining time just means nobody is waiting. Titles with an empty queue have been observed sitting on 1:22 and 1:45 while queued titles turned over every five minutes. The clock says nothing about how a title is granted — only whether anyone has applied.
  • No prerequisite has been observed — no might threshold, rank, or alliance requirement.
  • The title screen has a time-zone toggle at the top right. Switching it changes the header clock and the timestamps shown in every Apply queue, so a queue time read off this screen means whichever zone the toggle was set to. Check the toggle before quoting a handover time.

Why this matters for build planning

Chief of Affairs and Minister of Construction each grant +60% Building Construction Speed for free, to anyone willing to queue. On a long Sanctuary or building upgrade that is a very large saving, and it is the main reason observed build times in player guides differ so widely from the base figures. Queue for one before starting an expensive upgrade rather than after.

Two cannot be applied for; the other seven can

King of Battle is won — it goes to the leader of the alliance that takes the Royal City Scramble. Queen is appointed directly by the King. Neither has an Apply queue. Every other title, including Minister of Strategy, is open to any player through the Apply queue, each capped at 50 applicants. The King can additionally appoint the Chiefs directly; an in-game banner announcing exactly that was captured on 31 Jul 2026.

The two Chief titles are tied to KvK

If the server wins the weekly Kingdom-versus-Kingdom battle, it keeps Chief of Affairs and Chief of War. If it loses, those two titles are lost until the next battle — and whether the server was attacking or defending makes no difference. This matters more than it first looks: Chief of Affairs is the +60% construction and +60% research title, so a lost KvK week removes the strongest freely-available build buff on the server for everyone, not just for whoever held it.

Where the King comes from

The in-game Royal City Scramble rules state it plainly: the leader of the alliance that wins the Scramble becomes the King. That resolves the countdown seen on the Chief panels — “After 17:53, the title King of Battle will disappear” is the clock running down to the next Scramble, not a separate timer. The King’s own bonus panel is a flat +10% to hero HP, ATK and DEF plus +5% enemy troop death rate in PvP, and it is the one title with no Apply queue — it is won, not requested. Raven Epigraphs

Permanent squad bonuses that unlock late. Two guides cover this and they disagree in places — where they do, both readings are shown rather than one being picked.

What they are

Source
Three community infographics: “Raven Epigraph Guide 1/2”, “The Ultimate Guide to Raven Epigraphs”, and a third “Raven Epigraph Guide” that embeds in-game panel screenshots. Where the third reproduces an in-game panel, that panel is treated as in-game evidence and outranks the other two. The feature has not unlocked on server #217, so nothing here has been checked live. English-only for now.
  • Unlock 78 days after the server opens, through the Epigraph Workshop.
  • They follow rune mechanics — they do not act in direct combat, but grant permanent buffs to your squad or heroes.
  • Workshop level gates quality: Lv.1 processes level III epigraphs, Lv.15 unlocks SSR, Lv.20 unlocks UR.
  • Epigraphs cannot be transferred between factions, so match them to your main squad’s faction before investing.

Three things come out of Epigraph Chests

  • Epigraphs themselves — UR gold, SSR purple, SR blue.
  • Shards, which upgrade the Epigraph Workshop and unlock further features.
  • EXP Books, which are Raven-only. They evolve the Raven and cannot be spent on epigraphs at all — the guide calls this out twice, so it is evidently a common mistake. Low-quality epigraphs can also be fed into Raven evolution as materials.

Epigraphs come in the three factions

Faction Described as
WarriorOffensive — boosts ATK, Crit and damage output
RangerUtility and survivability — boosts HP, DEF and team durability
WarlockControl and support — boosts skills, cooldown reduction and effects

These are the same three factions the rest of the game uses, and the guide advises mixing them within a plan rather than going mono-faction. Note that this is a different axis from the bonus classes below — a given epigraph has both a faction and a class.

Two parallel mechanisms

  • Raven Evolve level. Feeding EXP Books raises the Raven’s stage and its own HP, ATK and DEF. Crucially it also drives the Epigraph Bonus, a global effect that applies on top of everything you have installed.
  • Epigraph Star level. Fuse two identical epigraphs to promote one to a higher star. The worked example given is a 2.5% Defense epigraph becoming 4.25% after the first combine.

What the Epigraph Bonus actually is

The in-game Epigraph Details panel states it directly: keep raising the Raven Evolve level and the Epigraph Bonus is enhanced at each uncap, with the next uncap shown as Lv.150. The panel displays it as a ladder of tiers — the captured example reads “when HP is above 50%, all allies’ damage taken is reduced by” 0% at the current tier, 1% at the next, 2% at the one after. So it is a squad-wide effect that grows in whole percentage points as the Raven levels, not a per-epigraph stat.

This settles the 150 / 300 / 450 question. One guide said those numbers unlock tier bonuses; another said they unlock epigraph plans. The in-game panel shows 150 is a Raven Evolve uncap that enhances the Epigraph Bonus, which matches the first reading. The second guide was not simply wrong, though — plan slots do also scale, just on their own track (see below). Two mechanisms, one set of numbers quoted for both.

Plans and plan slots

Each squad can run its own epigraph plan, and the plans themselves hold increasing numbers of epigraphs: the captured panel shows Plan 1 at 3 slots, Plan 2 at 4, Plan 3 at 5 and Plan 4 at 6, with Plan 4 still locked. More plans and more slots open as you progress.

Unresolved — what actually unlocks plan slots. The same guide says it two ways on one page: the plans panel says slots open as you evolve, while its own quick-tips list says to use Shards to upgrade the Epigraph Workshop and unlock more plan slots. Those are different currencies driving the same unlock. Both are recorded until a live panel settles it.

Shields

  • Shield before you log off, sleep, or stop checking. Without one, anyone can hit your city while you are away.
  • Shields do not protect troops outside your city. Bring marches home first, or they die regardless.
  • Buy from the Alliance Shop, not the diamond shop — the alliance 24-hour shield costs alliance currency and saves diamonds. The shop restocks weekly.
  • Re-shield before the current one expires, not after. Keep one or two spares of each duration in your bag.
  • Saturday is the day this matters most. Enemy kills score in the Alliance Duel Raid phase, so unshielded cities are actively hunted. Alliance policy for #217 is shield up all day until server reset, and no gathering at all — see the Kingdom vs Kingdom section.
  • Two places where a shield does nothing. Death Prison Blight waves ignore shield protection outright, and territories relocated into the Cursed Land around the Royal City cannot activate a shield at all. Plan troop positioning for both rather than relying on an item.
